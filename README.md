Three-dimensional visualization of segmented thoracic anatomy is challenging because adjacent and overlapping structures must remain distinguishable while preserving their anatomical context. Existing visualization approaches often use inconsistent color, opacity, and rendering settings, limiting the clarity and reproducibility of scientific visualizations.  We present SPECTRA (Standardized Perceptual Encoding of Clinical Three-dimensional Representation of Anatomy), an open-source framework for consistent visualization of segmented thoracic anatomy. SPECTRA generates two forms of animated three-dimensional visualization: orbital-view animations and exploded-view animations. Leveraging AI-assisted design, we developed perceptually coherent color lookup tables, distance-based opacity transfer functions for exploded-view animations and rendering parameters that emphasize scientific consistency, perceptual separability, anatomical grouping through color harmony, and preservation of anatomical context. When compared with an existing visualization preset, SPECTRA increased mean perceptual color separation from 12.63 to 33.95 using CIEDE2000 perceptual color differences. In addition to the visualization methodology, we provide the complete open-source toolkit with lookup tables, rendering presets, and example animated visualizations to encourage standardized three-dimensional scientific communication in medical imaging. 

The example visualizations are currently accessible at https://spectra.vandyvaliant.ai/
