Lung cancer is the leading cause of cancer deaths in the United States. To better understand the lungs and the respiratory system, lung atlases are used, and computed tomography (CT) scans are interpreted. However, it is becoming increasingly complicated to evaluate CT scans in a 3-dimensional way. Understanding CT scans involves complex knowledge, and there are often gaps between a 2-dimensional image and a 3-dimensional organ. To address this challenge, we developed an interactive, 3D, anatomical model of the lungs from cross sectional CT imaging. The open-source model provides a platform for understanding the spatial relationships of the lung segments and their airways. By creating the anatomical model directly from CT images, this platform can enhance education, support image interpretation, be utilized in surgical planning, and help communicate imaging findings to patients.   

Keywords: computed tomography, lung atlas, open-source, anatomical model
